Model assumptions & limitations

Ontario land transfer tax and Toronto municipal land transfer tax are modeled with progressive brackets, including illustrative first-time-buyer rebates ($4,000 Ontario and up to $4,475 Toronto) if eligible. These are editable as eligibility toggles, not automatic determinations. A spouse's prior ownership and occupancy can affect eligibility. Federal home buyers' amount, HST/new-build rebates, FHSA/HBP benefits, capital gains taxes, rental income tax, legal complexities and mortgage default insurance are not modeled. Closing-cost estimates and realtor commissions are editable. Selling costs are deducted when a property is sold. The condo-first path assumes the partner keeps the condo as a rental when the couple buys a house. Both paths purchase the same detached-house price at their respective purchase dates, with annual appreciation thereafter. Rent paid by one partner to the other is treated as an internal transfer for combined wealth. Investment contributions come from the same monthly household housing budget. If spending exceeds available cash, the model permits negative investment balances (borrowing), so verify affordability separately. Mortgage interest rates reset at the entered renewal year; investment growth and house-price appreciation are hypothetical. This planning model does not replace personalized tax, legal, or mortgage advice.
Model notes: In Scenario 1 the condo is retained as a rental when the detached house is purchased. Rental income is reduced for vacancy, management and an illustrative income-tax estimate; actual Canadian rental tax treatment, depreciation, capital gains and change-of-use elections require individual advice. Both scenarios use the same monthly household cash available for housing and investing. Negative investment balances indicate additional financing is required; this model does not charge interest on that shortfall. Mortgage qualification is estimated separately using editable assumptions; lender-specific approval is not guaranteed. First-time buyer eligibility is simplified and must be checked for your circumstances. Home equity excludes hypothetical future selling costs.
